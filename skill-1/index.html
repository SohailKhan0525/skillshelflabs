The diagnostic

Look for what makes the interface feel accidental.

Typography, surfaces, layout, states, motion, content, components, and code all get the same treatment: identify the weak pattern, then replace it with a focused alternative.

01

Typography

Consolidate the type system, strengthen hierarchy, remove weak weights, and give headlines room to lead.

02

Color & surfaces

Remove noisy gradients, commit to the approved neutrals, and add depth through texture and considered lighting.

03

Layout

Break predictable symmetry, constrain the reading measure, and use spacing as part of the visual hierarchy.

04

States & motion

Make hover, focus, loading, empty, error, and scroll states feel designed rather than appended.

The principle

Improve what exists.
Do not rewrite for the sake of rewriting.
