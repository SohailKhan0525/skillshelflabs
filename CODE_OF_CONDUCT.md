# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

SkillShelf is a community project. Be respectful, constructive, and honest.

We expect contributors to:

- communicate professionally;
- respect creators and their attribution;
- avoid harassment or discrimination;
- report problems in good faith;
- avoid deliberately publishing misleading or fabricated catalog information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or contributions that violate these standards or materially harm the community.

For sensitive concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately through GitHub.
